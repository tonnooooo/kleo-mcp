Your car key is on the kitchen bench. And your car is already gone. Under 30 seconds. No broken glass. It is called a relay attack. Two thieves, one cheap amplifier. One stands at your front door and boosts the signal of the key inside your house. The other one stands at your car. The car thinks the key is right there, and it starts. ADAC tested 850 keyless cars. 85 percent were opened and driven away. The fix is a Faraday pouch. Drop the key in, and the signal dies. No signal, no relay, and no stolen car.
